The Quiet Dawn

When morning paints the silent sky,
The stars retreat without goodbye.
A gentle breeze begins to sing,
Awakening the heart of spring.

Each step we take, though small it seems,
Is stitched together with our dreams.
For every storm that clouds the view,
The sun still finds a way shining through.

So walk with hope, embrace the climb,
Let patience blossom over time.
The brightest paths are often made
By those who walk, though unafraid.
